⚖️ Statutory Gazette References

Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011

Rule 6(1)(h): Mandatory declaration of retail sale price inclusive of all taxes with Unit Sale Price (USP) as per G.S.R. 629(E).

Table-I Schedule: Mandatory minimum numeral heights based on packaging principal display panel (PDP) area (e.g., 2.0mm for net qty up to 200g).

Jan Vishwas (Amendment) Act, 2023

Act No. 18 of 2023 (Sec 36(1) Proviso): Complete decriminalization of first-time procedural label defects. Mandates an immediate 15-Day Statutory Improvement Notice at ₹0 compounding fee before any civil compounding penalty.

Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA 2023)

Section 63 (Repealed Sec 65B): Admissibility of electronic records requires cryptographic hash integrity, tamper-evident device custody, and monotonic timestamp verification.

Metrological Traceability

ISO/IEC 17025 & JCGM 100:2008 (GUM): Sub-millimeter planar homography calibration with 95% expanded coverage factor ($k=2, \pm 0.08\text{ mm}$).
