## 📜 Statutory Attribution Policy

All contributions strictly adhere to:
1. **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Adjudication:** The system provides statutory diagnostics to assist authorized Legal Metrology Officers; it does not issue automated penalties.
2. **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA 2023):** Electronic records generated under Section 63 evidentiary standards.
3. **Open Source Compliance:** Zero AGPL-3.0 dependencies; strictly Apache-2.0, MIT, BSD-3-Clause, and PostgreSQL permissive licensing.
