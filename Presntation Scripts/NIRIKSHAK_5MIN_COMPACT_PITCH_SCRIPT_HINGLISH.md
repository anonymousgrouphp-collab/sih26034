## PART 2: THE CORE TECH (Calibration Math, Vision & Deterministic AST)

**⏱️ 1:00 – 3:00 (120 Seconds) | Budget: ~230 Words**

_(Stage Cue: Point to Pipeline architecture slide)_

### [1:00 - 1:30] Why Zero Generative AI in Legal Decisions?

> _"Sabse pehle: **'Kya AI hallucinate karke galat notice bhejega?'**  
> Hamara answer hai: **Bilkul nahi!**  
> Generative LLMs probabilistic hote hain — unpar legal penalties decide nahi ho sakti.  
> NIRIKSHAK mein Deep Learning sirf character aur polygon detection tak simit hai. Legal compliance hamara **Deterministic AST Rule Engine** karta hai — exact Gazette clauses, zero hallucination!"_

### [1:30 - 2:15] Innovations 1 & 2: Planar Homography & Multilingual Edge Vision

_(Stage Cue: Show reference card / calibration visual)_

> **[OPTION A - Direct Math & Caliper Precision]:**  
> \*"**Pehli Innovation: Planar Homography Calibration.**  
> 2D photo mein pixels hote hain, millimeters nahi. Inspector product ke sath standard ISO-7810 card ya ArUco marker rakhta hai. Hamara system **3x3 Homography Matrix ($H$)** se perspective tilt un-tilt karta hai aur pixels ko exact real-world millimeters mein convert karta hai.  
> Real physical test mein digital caliper reading **1.47 mm** thi — Nirikshak ne measure kiya **1.46 mm** — delta sirf **0.01 mm**!
>
> **Doosri Innovation: Multilingual Edge Vision.**  
> DBNet++ aur PP-OCRv4 INT8 quantized ONNX models standard CPU par English aur Devanagari Hindi ko sirf **180 milliseconds** mein padhte hain — with **₹0 Cloud GPU expense**."\*

> **[OR OPTION B - Algorithmic Rigor]:**  
> _"Linear pixel scaling tilt hone par fail ho jaati hai. Hum 4 coplanar points se Planar Homography calculate karke perspective skew ko geometrically rectify karte hain — achieving sub-0.30mm verified accuracy.  
> Multilingual OCR ke liye hum DBNet++ aur PP-OCRv4 CPU-quantized pipeline use karte hain, jo Latin aur Devanagari Hindi dono scripts ko 180ms mein local hardware par transcribe karti hai."_

### [2:15 - 3:00] Innovations 3 & 4: 3D Semantic Fusion & Section 63 BSA Vault

_(Stage Cue: Point to 3D multi-panel package and PDF certificate)_

> \*"**Teesri Innovation: Cross-Facet Semantic Fusion.**  
> Packages 3D hote hain — brand aage hai, MRP side mein, consumer care peeche. Inspector multi-angle photos leta hai, aur Fusion Engine spatial graph se saare tokens ko link karke composite legal docket assemble karta hai.
>
> **Chauthi Innovation: Section 63 BSA 2023 Evidentiary Vault.**  
> Har raw camera buffer, GPS coordinate, aur device fingerprint ka **SHA-256 Merkle DAG Root** banta hai, jo official Form-1 Notice PDF mein digitally seal hota hai. Image ka ek single pixel bhi tamper hone par cryptographic certificate reject ho jayega. Courtroom mein evidence undeniable hai!"\*
